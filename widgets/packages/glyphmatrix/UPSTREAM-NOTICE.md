Original Nothing KDE widget (not from upstream). Code: GPL-3.0-or-later.
The round 25 x 25 matrix follows the shape of the Nothing Phone (3)'s Glyph
Matrix; no Nothing code or artwork is included. Glyph Toys are Android apps
and cannot run here; pictures and GIFs can be shown instead.
The dot-matrix font is Nothing Dots (SIL OFL 1.1, from Bitcount Single;
see contents/ui/glassy/fonts/NothingDots-OFL.txt).
