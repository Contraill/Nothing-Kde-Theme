Adapted for Nothing KDE from Jack Faith / jaxparrow07/nothing-kde-widgets.
Code: GPL-3.0. Original author attribution is preserved in metadata.
Panel compact representations removed; defaults follow KDE mode/accent.
The dot-matrix font is Nothing Dots (SIL OFL 1.1, from Bitcount Single;
see contents/ui/glassy/fonts/NothingDots-OFL.txt). No upstream font remains: the
serif is Nothing Serif (SIL OFL 1.1, from Fraunces), the large clock's dotted, tiles
and stencil faces are drawn for Nothing KDE (SIL OFL 1.1, see the large clock's
contents/fonts/NothingClockFaces-OFL.txt) and Roboto is Apache 2.0.

Some styles are designs from EveryThing-UI by Prince-1652 (MIT; notice in
contents/ui/glassy/EveryThing-UI-LICENSE.txt), redrawn in contents/ui/everything/.
